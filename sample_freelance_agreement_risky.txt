INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Brightpeak Digital
Solutions LLC, a company organized under the laws of Delaware ("Company"),
and the undersigned freelance contractor ("Contractor"). Company and
Contractor may each be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the
"Parties."

WHEREAS, Company desires to engage Contractor to perform certain design
and development services, and Contractor desires to provide such
services, the Parties agree as follows:

SECTION 1. SERVICES
1.1 Contractor shall perform web design, front-end development, and
related digital services as described in Exhibit A (the "Services") to
the satisfaction of Company, in Company's sole and absolute discretion.
1.2 Company reserves the right to modify the scope of Services at any
time without adjustment to the compensation set forth in Section 3,
unless such modification exceeds fifty percent (50%) of the original
scope, as determined by Company.

SECTION 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
2.1 Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to
Company, in perpetuity and throughout the universe, all right, title, and
interest in and to any and all work product, inventions, designs, code,
concepts, drafts, notes, and derivative works created by Contractor at
any time during the Term, whether or not related to the Services,
including any pre-existing intellectual property incorporated into the
deliverables.
2.2 Contractor waives any and all moral rights in the work product and
agrees not to use, license, or reference any work product created under
this Agreement in Contractor's portfolio, case studies, or marketing
materials without Company's prior written consent, which may be withheld
in Company's sole discretion.

SECTION 3. COMPENSATION
3.1 Company shall pay Contractor a total fee of $8,500 for the Services,
payable within ninety (90) days following Company's final written
acceptance of all deliverables.
3.2 No interest, penalty, or late fee shall accrue on any payment made by
Company, regardless of delay.
3.3 Company may withhold payment in full if, in Company's sole judgment,
any deliverable requires revision, with no obligation to specify the
required revisions in writing.

SECTION 4. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
4.1 Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Company, its
officers, directors, employees, and affiliates from and against any and
all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including
reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to the Services,
this Agreement, or Contractor's acts or omissions, without limitation as
to amount.
4.2 Company's total liability to Contractor under this Agreement, whether
in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed one hundred dollars
($100), under any circumstances.
4.3 Contractor's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited and
shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

SECTION 5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
5.1 During the Term and for a period of five (5) years following
termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall not, directly or
indirectly, provide services similar to the Services to any business
operating in the digital design, marketing, or software development
industry anywhere in the world.
5.2 Contractor shall not solicit, hire, or engage any employee,
contractor, or client of Company for a period of five (5) years following
termination, regardless of who initiates contact.

SECTION 6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Contractor shall keep confidential all information disclosed by
Company, whether marked confidential or not, indefinitely, with no
expiration of this obligation.

SECTION 7. TERMINATION
7.1 Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without
cause, immediately upon written notice to Contractor.
7.2 Contractor may terminate this Agreement only upon ninety (90) days'
prior written notice to Company, and only after completing all
outstanding deliverables in progress at the time notice is given.
7.3 Upon termination for any reason, Contractor shall not be entitled to
payment for any work performed but not yet accepted by Company under
Section 3.3.

SECTION 8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.
8.2 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively
through binding arbitration administered by an arbitrator selected solely
by Company, at a location of Company's choosing. Contractor waives any
right to a jury trial or class action.

SECTION 9. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
9.1 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year
terms unless Contractor provides written notice of non-renewal at least
one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

SECTION 10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements,
whether written or oral.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the
Effective Date first written above.
