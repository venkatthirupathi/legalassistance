INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Meridian Studio
Collective LLC, a company organized under the laws of California
("Company"), and the undersigned freelance contractor ("Contractor").
Company and Contractor may each be referred to as a "Party" and
collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, Company desires to engage Contractor to perform certain design
and development services, and Contractor desires to provide such
services, the Parties agree as follows:

SECTION 1. SERVICES
1.1 Contractor shall perform web design, front-end development, and
related digital services as described in Exhibit A (the "Services").
1.2 Any material change to the scope of Services shall be documented in
a written change order signed by both Parties, including any associated
adjustment to compensation or timeline.

SECTION 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
2.1 Upon full payment of all fees due under this Agreement, Contractor
assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in the final
deliverables specifically created for Company under this Agreement.
2.2 Contractor retains ownership of any pre-existing tools, libraries,
frameworks, or components used in creating the deliverables, and grants
Company a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use such pre-existing
materials solely as incorporated into the deliverables.
2.3 Contractor may reference the completed project in Contractor's
portfolio and marketing materials, provided that no confidential
business information of Company is disclosed.

SECTION 3. COMPENSATION
3.1 Company shall pay Contractor a total fee of $8,500 for the Services,
payable in two installments: fifty percent (50%) upon signing this
Agreement, and the remaining fifty percent (50%) within fourteen (14)
days following delivery of the final deliverables.
3.2 Any payment not made within fourteen (14) days of its due date shall
accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month.
3.3 If Company requests revisions, Company shall provide specific written
feedback within five (5) business days, and Contractor shall have a
reasonable opportunity to address such feedback before any payment is
withheld.

SECTION 4. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
4.1 Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from
claims arising out of that Party's own negligence, willful misconduct, or
material breach of this Agreement.
4.2 Neither Party's total liability under this Agreement shall exceed the
total fees paid or payable under this Agreement, except in cases of gross
negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations.

SECTION 5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
5.1 During the Term of this Agreement, Contractor agrees not to perform
substantially similar services for Company's direct competitors on the
same project scope.
5.2 For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, neither
Party shall solicit the other's employees for the purpose of hiring them
away, though this shall not restrict Contractor's ability to work with
any client, including Company's competitors, in Contractor's ordinary
course of business.

SECTION 6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential any proprietary information
disclosed by the other Party and marked as confidential, for a period of
three (3) years following disclosure.

SECTION 7. TERMINATION
7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon
fourteen (14) days' written notice to the other Party.
7.2 Either Party may terminate immediately for material breach that
remains uncured ten (10) business days after written notice of the
breach.
7.3 Upon termination, Contractor shall be paid for all work satisfactorily
completed up to the effective date of termination, on a pro-rata basis.

SECTION 8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
California.
8.2 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be addressed
through good-faith negotiation between the Parties, and if unresolved
within thirty (30) days, may be submitted to mediation before either
Party pursues litigation or arbitration in a mutually agreed venue.

SECTION 9. TERM
9.1 This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues
until the Services are completed and accepted, unless earlier terminated
under Section 7. This Agreement does not automatically renew.

SECTION 10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements,
whether written or oral.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the
Effective Date first written above.
